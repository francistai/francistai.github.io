Executive Profile

Executive leader with over 30 years of international OEM/ODM leadership across product development, manufacturing, global supply chain, commercial operations, project execution, and P&L management. Proven track record delivering innovative products from concept through commercialization across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and North America, serving multinational corporations, publicly listed companies, SMEs, and technology startups across multiple industries, including consumer electronics, connected devices, Edge AI, and IoT. Recognized for business transformation, operational excellence, strategic sourcing, product commercialization, and leading cross-functional organizations to deliver scalable, manufacturing-ready products.

Throughout my career, I was entrusted with progressively broader executive leadership responsibilities, typically within six months, in recognition of my leadership, execution excellence, and business performance. My responsibilities expanded across corporate strategy, global operations, product commercialization, project execution, manufacturing, global supply chain, and P&L management, reflecting sustained executive growth and progressively broader organizational leadership throughout my career.

From the beginning of my engineering career, I was selected to join one of my company's first 300,000+ unit Sony & Sansui OEM product development programs under the technical leadership of a contract-based Japanese Chief Engineer with extensive engineering experience at Panasonic Japan. This early opportunity immersed me in Japanese engineering discipline, multinational collaboration, structured product development, and rigorous execution standards while serving as a key technical interface between company management, Japanese engineering leadership, Sony & Sansui Business Units across multiple countries and regions, chipset and technology partners, and cross-functional Engineering, Procurement, Manufacturing, and Quality teams.

Beyond my direct executive responsibilities, I was also entrusted by senior executives and publicly listed companies to lead strategic projects, business transformation, and cross-functional execution across multiple industries in Singapore, Indonesia, and Malaysia, reflecting confidence in my leadership, execution capability, and ability to deliver measurable business results across diverse business environments. Most recently, I led the strategy, system architecture, commercialization, and government innovation funding initiatives for an Edge AI-enabled IoT platform, including NRC IRAP, SR&ED, and BDC innovation and commercialization programs.

Execution speed, data-driven decision-making, and measurable business results are at the core of my leadership philosophy. I transform strategy into execution, build high-performing teams, and accelerate product commercialization, manufacturing readiness, and sustainable business growth.
